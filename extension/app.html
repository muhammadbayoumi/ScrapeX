Remove or erase

Two different things. Read which one you want.

Stop tracking this source

Takes it off the crawl list. Every row it has already collected stays — the prices, the history and the crawl log are a record of what the shop published, and removing the entry does not undo that.

Erase its collected data

Deletes every row this source ever ingested and KEEPS the source itself, so the next run starts clean. A backup is taken first, always.
