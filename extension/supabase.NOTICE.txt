SUPABASE DESIGN SYSTEM — ATTRIBUTION AND STATEMENT OF CHANGES
============================================================

WHAT THIS FILE IS FOR

design/tokens.css carries colour values, and the expressions behind them, that
were read from Supabase's design system. Ruling R-74 makes that system this
product's baseline rather than one option among several, so the borrowing is
structural and not incidental, and it is owed a notice.

This file discharges that. It is carried into every distribution of the design
assets by tools/sync_design_assets.py, and a guard in tests/test_design_system.py
fails the build if a copy goes missing.

STATEMENT OF CHANGES — Apache License 2.0, section 4(b)

The values in design/tokens.css are CHANGED from what Supabase publishes. Every
change is recorded at the value in that file; this is the summary.

  1. RESOLVED, NOT COPIED. Their semantic colours are computed at runtime in
     OKLCH from a small set of scalar inputs per theme. This product resolves
     those expressions to static values, because it ships CSS without a build
     step. The arithmetic is theirs; the constants are the output of it.

  2. FIVE VALUES WERE DELIBERATELY REPLACED, each because it fails a contrast
     floor this product enforces and theirs does not state:

       --line-strong   their --border-stronger is 1.57:1 on their own card
       --amber         their --warning is 2.68:1 on their own warning-300 tint
       --focus         their focus ring flattens to 1.47:1 on their background
       --accent-contrast   their brand green is 1.99:1 against white
       device --accent-contrast   the on-colour their OKLCH-lightness step picks
                       is near-white where WCAG relative luminance wants black,
                       because the two disagree about saturated blues

  3. NAMES DIVERGE ABOVE THE BODY SIZE. This product's type scale carries an
     `md` step that Tailwind has no name for, so from --fs-md upward every rung
     sits one place below theirs. The values match; the names do not.

  4. THE MOTION VOCABULARY IS NOT THEIRS. Neither easing curve in
     design/tokens.css appears in their source, and their most-used duration has
     no token here.

  5. TOKENS THEY DO NOT HAVE. Shadow tokens, line-height tokens, a forced-colors
     block, a prefers-contrast accommodation, and a bidirectional-text contract
     are this product's own. Supabase publishes none of them.

  6. THE ICON SET IS THEIRS IN NAME ONLY, AND THIS IS THE ONE CHANGE HERE THAT IS
     NOT A COLOUR. Supabase mandates Lucide at size 24, strokeWidth 1.5, stroke
     currentColor and fill none. This product ships a curated Google Material
     sprite instead: 50 symbols, 37 of them referenced, across 173 call sites,
     filled rather than stroked. The owner recorded the difference rather than
     migrating it, on 2026-09-02, because the ruling that adopts their system
     adopts its VALUES and an icon set is not a value. The Material sprite carries
     its own Apache-2.0 notice in three places in this repository, and that notice
     is separate from this one.

  7. THE BIDIRECTIONAL CONTRACT IS EXEMPT BY RULING, NOT BY OVERSIGHT. Their
     system is authored left-to-right: 268 physical directional properties against
     6 logical across 351 files, no rtl variants, no direction provider, and no
     mention of bidirectional text in any of their 105 documentation files. Half
     of this product's collected data is Arabic, so on that axis there is nothing
     of theirs to copy and matching them could only mean deleting what exists
     here. 190 logical properties, 22 bidi-control declarations and an Arabic face
     in both font stacks are kept deliberately.


WHAT IS NOT COVERED BY THIS NOTICE

Supabase's brand marks, their wordmark, and their licensed typeface are not used
here and are not distributed with this product. design/tokens.css names a font
STACK of freely available faces; it does not ship theirs.
