SUPABASE DESIGN SYSTEM — ATTRIBUTION AND STATEMENT OF CHANGES
============================================================

WHAT THIS FILE IS FOR

design/tokens.css carries colour values, and the expressions behind them, that
were read from Supabase's design system. Ruling R-74 makes that system this
product's baseline rather than one option among several, so the borrowing is
structural and not incidental, and it is owed a notice.

This file discharges that. It is carried into every distribution of the design
assets by tools/sync_design_assets.py, and a guard in tests/test_design_system.py
fails the build if a copy goes missing.

STATEMENT OF CHANGES — Apache License 2.0, section 4(b)

Most values in design/tokens.css are Supabase's; a few are not, and the shape of
the borrowing differs from theirs in ways that are not values at all. Section 4(b)
asks for a statement of what changed, so this is that statement. Every change is
also recorded at the value in design/tokens.css, and that file is authoritative
where this summary is brief.

  1. RESOLVED, NOT COPIED. Their semantic colours are computed at runtime in
     OKLCH from a small set of scalar inputs per theme. This product resolves
     those expressions to static values, because it ships CSS without a build
     step. The arithmetic is theirs; the constants are the output of it.

  2. NO COLOUR VALUE IS DELIBERATELY REPLACED. This item listed five, and every
     one of the five is gone. It is kept as an empty item rather than deleted,
     because a reader comparing this notice against an earlier distribution needs
     to know the list emptied rather than moved.

     THREE WERE RESTORED. --line-strong, --amber and --focus were replacements
     until 2026-08-31, when R-85 ruled that this system matches Supabase exactly.
     A FOURTH DESCRIBED A PATH THAT NO LONGER EXISTS: an on-colour for device
     colour mode, which R-85 deleted entirely.

     AND THE FIFTH WAS NEVER A REPLACEMENT, which took two review passes to find.
     --accent-contrast ships #030303 in light and #131413 in dark, and this item
     said that was a deliberate near-black chosen because their brand green
     measures 1.99:1 against white. Both values are in fact Supabase's own
     resolved output. Their --primary-foreground is

         oklch( min(--surface, --foreground-lightness)
                calc(--chroma * 0.45)
                --hue )

     and evaluating it with the scalars their own theme files declare at the
     commit above gives exactly those two values: light surface 0.995 and
     foreground-lightness 0.1 and chroma 0 give oklch(0.1 0 159) = #030303, and
     dark surface 0.19 and foreground-lightness 0.95 and chroma 0.005 give
     oklch(0.19 0.00225 159) = #131413. Supabase reaches the same near-black by
     the same reasoning; this product did not depart from them, it agreed with
     them. The contrast measurement is corroboration and was never the cause.

     So every colour value here belongs to item 1, resolved from their arithmetic
     and not chosen against it, WITH THESE EXCEPTIONS, which a statement of
     changes must name. The Sign in with Google button's colours, three per
     theme, are Google's published colours, not Supabase's. --red-hover, in both
     themes, is this product's own: Supabase publishes neither #8e332f nor
     #fda49d. The dark --accent-active is their light --brand-500 used in the
     dark theme, a value of theirs they do not use there. --shadow-color, in both
     themes, is this product's own, because Supabase declares no shadow token; it
     belongs with the shadow tokens item 5 names. And --selection and the five
     --diagram-* colours are this product's own color-mix() of their resolved
     values, at proportions Supabase does not publish. This sentence claimed no
     exception until 2026-09-25. The contrast numbers the removed entries carried
     are still recorded at the values in design/tokens.css, which is where a reader who
     wonders why a border measures 1.542:1 finds both the number and the ruling.

  3. THE TYPE SCALE'S VALUES ARE THEIRS BUT ONE, AND ITS NAMES ARE NOT. Measured
     against apps/design-system/styles/globals.css at the commit above, whose @theme
     block re-declares Tailwind's --text-sm through --text-9xl and leaves --text-xs
     at Tailwind's stock 0.75rem: --fs-xs (12px) is their --text-xs and --fs-sm
     (13px) their --text-sm; --fs (15px) is their --text-base; and from --fs-md
     upward (16, 18, 22, 28px) each rung is theirs one name higher, --text-lg to
     --text-3xl, because this product calls its 15px rung --fs and gives 16px an
     `md` name that Tailwind does not have. --fs-2xs (11px) has no counterpart in
     their ramp or in the stock ramp their mono context resets to.
     THIS ITEM SAID "THE VALUES MATCH" UNTIL 2026-09-25; --fs-2xs did not.

  4. THE MOTION CURVES ARE THEIRS; NAMING THEM IS NOT. Both curves in
     design/tokens.css are Supabase's own, at the commit above, in
     packages/config/css/animations.css: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) in four of
     their --animate-* declarations and cubic-bezier(0.87, 0, 0.13, 1) in eight.
     Three of their durations are here too, 0.1s and 0.15s and 0.25s. What is this
     product's own is that the curves are ADDRESSABLE: they declare no custom
     property for either and inline both inside animation shorthands, while
     --ease and --ease-travel make them referable. Their most-used duration,
     300ms, has no rung here.

     THIS ITEM CLAIMED THE OPPOSITE UNTIL 2026-09-07 -- that neither curve
     appeared in their source. It was wrong in the direction a licence notice can
     least afford, crediting this product with their work.

  5. TOKENS AND ACCOMMODATIONS THEY DO NOT HAVE. Shadow tokens, line-height
     tokens and a prefers-contrast accommodation are in design/tokens.css. A
     forced-colors block and a bidirectional-text contract -- unicode-bidi
     isolation plus logical box and alignment properties -- are in
     design/components.css. Searching their repository for forced-colors,
     prefers-contrast and unicode-bidi returns nothing at all, so none of the five
     is theirs. The heading of this section names tokens.css; two of the five live
     one file over, and saying so costs less than letting a reader discover it.

     REDUCED MOTION IS NOT ON THIS LIST, and it was on an earlier draft of it.
     Supabase does honour prefers-reduced-motion -- fourteen places in their
     repository, including packages/ui-patterns -- so respecting it here is not a
     divergence and does not belong in a statement of changes.

     THE BIDIRECTIONAL CONTRACT IS ALSO THE ONE AXIS THE EXACTNESS RULING
     EXEMPTS. R-85 makes this system Supabase's exactly, and he scoped that to the
     value layer. Their system is written in physical directions and their
     documentation does not raise bidirectional text, while half of what this
     product collects is Arabic. Matching them on that axis could only mean
     deleting the contract, so it is exempt by ruling and not by oversight.

  6. THE ICON SET IS NOT THEIRS, and it is recorded here until it is repaired.
     Supabase's UI icons are Lucide (apps/design-system/content/docs/icons.mdx at
     the commit above), which specifies no size or stroke for them. A Lucide icon
     renders at Lucide's own defaults (size 24, stroke 2, currentColor, no fill)
     unless the component holding it or the call site overrides them, and where
     their components override the stroke they mostly set 1.5. Their own custom
     icons, for glyphs Lucide lacks, default to strokeWidth 1.5 where Lucide
     defaults to 2; both default to size 24. This product ships a Material Design
     sprite, design/material-icons.svg -- Google's classic filled Material Icons
     plus one Material Symbols Outlined glyph, folder-code -- with Google's own
     Apache-2.0 licence file beside each copy. Its counts are not
     restated here, for the reason WHAT CAME ACROSS gives: a count is a
     restatement.

     ASKED ON 2026-09-02 WHETHER TO MIGRATE OR TO RECORD IT, HE CHOSE TO RECORD
     IT. R-85's exactness instruction was scoped to the values, and an icon set is
     not a colour value. His ruling of 2026-09-23 reversed that: the product moves
     to Lucide, and this item is rewritten when it does.


WHAT IS NOT COVERED BY THIS NOTICE

Supabase's brand marks, their wordmark, and their licensed typeface are not used
here and are not distributed with this product. design/tokens.css names a font
STACK of freely available faces; it does not ship theirs.
