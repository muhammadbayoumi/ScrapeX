How ScrapeX works

A record keeps its path from source to answer

The price branch and the general-data branch share one rule: preserve what the source said before adding your own interpretation.

  1. 01 Register a source

    source_site or site_profile identifies where the data comes from.

  2. 02 Capture evidence

    Runs and snapshots preserve when, where, and how the source was read.

  3. 03 Keep source identity

    Products, variants, datasets, and fields stay in the source's own vocabulary.

  4. 04 Record facts

    Offers and observations store price facts; generic records store flexible non-price data.

  5. 05 Build history

    Periods, revisions, and change events explain what changed and when.

  6. 06 Curate and publish

    Reviewed matches and saved views turn preserved facts into tables, reports, Excel, and synchronization outputs.

Model rules

Why the model is structured this way

01

Source facts stay source facts

Names, classifications, prices, and evidence are preserved before any matching or normalization.

02

History is append-only

A new observation adds evidence. It never overwrites the price that was true before it.

03

Human review joins worlds

Source products reach the unified catalogue through reviewed match tables, not an irreversible automatic merge.

04

Every answer keeps lineage

Runs, snapshots, jobs, and logs explain where a stored fact came from and what produced it.
